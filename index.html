What We Design

Engineering Services

We design high-power electrical systems end to end: the power electronics, the motor, and the controls that tie them together. Engage us for a single subsystem or a complete drive.

01

Power Electronics

High-power inverters and converters at 800 V and beyond: SiC power stages, gate drives, busbars and power-loop layout, and protection, designed for density and robustness.

02

Electric Motor Design

Motors designed alongside the drive that powers them, with electromagnetic and thermal design matched to your torque, speed, and packaging targets.

03

Controls & Firmware

Motor control and inverter firmware for servo and traction drives, with EtherCAT, CAN, and Ethernet communications and resolver or encoder feedback.

04

Thermal & Validation

Liquid-cooled thermal design, EMI/EMC design and pre-compliance, and hardware test and validation against your requirements.

How We Work

Concept to Production

Work with us on a single project or as a long-term engineering partner. Either way, the path from requirements to hardware is the same.

01

Discovery

Share your power, voltage, thermal, and packaging requirements. We respond within 48 hours with an initial read on fit and a proposed path to hardware.

02

Architecture

Topology, device, thermal, and motor trade studies up front, so the design is right before hardware is built.

03

Rapid Prototyping

Fast design-build-test loops against your requirements, with hardware engineered from first principles for high-shock, high-vibration, mission-critical environments.

04

Production

Design for manufacture and hand-off to manufacturing partners, with engineering support as your program scales.
