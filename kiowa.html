The Architecture

Why Module-Less

The Problem

Conventional power modules put a thermal resistance stack between die and coolant: substrate, solder, thermal interface material, and baseplate. Each layer adds °C/W. At high power density the module becomes the bottleneck, not the device, and the package sets the size.

The Kiowa Approach

Kiowa eliminates the module entirely. Silicon carbide die mount directly to a precision-machined cold plate, removing multiple layers of thermal resistance. That shorter thermal path is what makes over 100 kW/L possible at 800 V.

→ Density by design

Power density is a product of the architecture, not a packaging exercise applied to a commodity power module.

Scalable by Design

Built to Adapt

Kiowa's architecture scales from 100 kW to 500 kW and beyond. Size and cost track the power rating rather than forcing every program into a one-size-fits-all box, and qualification work carries forward from prototype to production.

FAQ

About Kiowa

What is Kiowa?

Kiowa is an 800 V silicon carbide inverter platform from SiCore Research. It uses a module-less, direct-die architecture to reach over 100 kW/L peak power density, and one unit can serve as a servo drive, traction drive, or power-export converter.

What power levels does Kiowa cover?

The architecture scales from 100 kW to 500 kW and beyond, with size and cost tracking the power rating.

What interfaces does Kiowa support?

CAN, Ethernet, and EtherCAT communications, with resolver, incremental encoder, or absolute encoder position feedback.

Can Kiowa be adapted to our program?

Yes. Kiowa is designed to adapt to the motor, network, and power level each application demands, and our engineering services team can tailor it or design around it.
